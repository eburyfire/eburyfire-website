# Gas suppression in a server room: what maintenance covers

You test your backups. You patch your servers. The cylinders in the corner of the comms room deserve the same attention.

A gas suppression system puts out a fire by filling the room with an extinguishing gas, without water anywhere near the kit. It only works if three things are true on the day: the gas is there, the detection fires it, and the room holds the gas long enough. Maintenance checks all three.

The standard for gas suppression is BS EN 15004. It's a different standard from the one for fire alarms (BS 5839-1), and it needs someone who knows suppression systems.

## Is the gas there?

Each cylinder is checked for contents and pressure, then for damage, corrosion and secure mounting. A cylinder that has quietly lost pressure is a system that won't work, and you won't know from looking at it.

If your system uses an HFC agent, such as HFC-227ea, the F-gas rules can add leak checks and records of their own.

## Will it go off when it should?

- the detectors are tested, often set up so two must agree before the gas releases
- the control panel is checked for faults and for the right sequence: alarm, countdown, release
- the hold and abort controls are checked, so someone in the room can stop a release
- the manual release is checked
- sounders and warning lights inside and outside the room are tested

## Will the room hold the gas?

This is the bit that changes without anyone noticing. A new cable run through the wall, a ceiling tile left out, a gap under a new door: each one is a way for the gas to leak out before it has done its job.

The room is checked for new openings, and a room integrity test (often a door fan test) shows whether it still holds the gas for as long as the design needs.

## Signs and the people in the room

Warning signs on every door, instructions by the controls, and staff who know what the countdown means and where the abort button is. These get checked too.

## What you're left with

A service report and certificate, a list of anything that needs fixing, quoted line by line, and the records in your customer portal.
