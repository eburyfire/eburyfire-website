# Big name or small firm: questions to ask any fire alarm provider

A big name isn't the same as a good service. To a national firm you can be one account among thousands. At the other end, a man with a van can be cheap right up until you need the certificate.

Whoever looks after your fire alarm now, these six questions tell you more than the logo on the van.

## 1. Where is my last certificate, right now?

You should be able to find it in two minutes. If it's in an email from last year, or a drawer, or "the office has it", that's your answer.

## 2. Is my price fixed for the year, in writing?

Servicing should be one agreed price. Extra work, such as replacing a detector, should be quoted before it's done, not discovered on an invoice.

## 3. What exactly gets tested at each visit?

Ask for the list. A proper service checks the paperwork, every device, the sounders, the panel and the batteries, and leaves the log book up to date. Our guide to [what a service visit should cover](/articles/annual-maintenance-checks) has the full list.

## 4. Who do I ring, and do they know my building?

A call centre that has to look you up isn't the same as one named contact who knows your buildings.

## 5. How many firms look after my buildings?

Fire alarm with one firm, gas suppression with another, a different firm per site? Each comes with its own paperwork and its own idea of when the next visit is due. One contract can cover the lot.

## 6. What happens when something's found?

You should be told before the visit ends what was tested and what was found, with a quote for anything that needs fixing, line by line.
